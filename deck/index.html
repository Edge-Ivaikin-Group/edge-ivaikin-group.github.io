First stage

Agree how you’ll accept the work before we build.

  1. Scope one workflow

    Identify the people, systems and access involved. Agree the deliverable, budget and what must be true for you to accept it.

  2. Demonstrate it end to end

    Walk through the agreed workflow and review it against the acceptance criteria and the metric chosen for the pilot.

  3. Decide what to do next

    Use the review to choose whether to extend the workflow, move into ongoing delivery or conclude the engagement.

A narrowly scoped pilot is typically estimated at 2–6 weeks, depending on scope, system access and integrations. See published starting prices.
