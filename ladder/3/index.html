Step 3 of 7 · The approach

How the work is organised

A simple ladder: prove value fast, then scale.

Engagement model

1
Pilot MVP (2–6 weeks)
Single metric, demo, and decision-ready artifact.
2
Monthly retainer
Roadmap + continuous delivery + measurable improvements.
3
Productize
Turn proven solutions into repeatable delivery for adjacent clients.

Start with one business process

1
Describe the bottleneck
What gets stuck, which systems are involved, and what needs to change.
2
Define a bounded first stage
Agree scope, dependencies, budget and acceptance criteria before development.
3
Decide what to scale
Review the delivered stage against the agreed criteria, then choose the next step.

How delivery is run

Delivery that survives audits, hand-offs, and scale.

Clear contracts

Defined scope, SLAs, and deliverables.

Clean IP hygiene

Client-owned deliverables, repository discipline, access control.

Transparent reporting

Monthly numbers, docs, and evidence.
