Before we start

Do we need a finished specification?

No. Start with the current process, the systems involved and the result you want. Scope and dependencies are clarified before development.

Can you work with our existing systems and team?

Integration, platforms and delivery support are part of our services. Feasibility depends on system access, API limits and the responsibilities agreed with your team.

What determines the price and timeline?

Scope, integrations, data quality and acceptance requirements. The published prices are starting points; the final scope, cost and timeline are agreed separately.

Tell us what needs to change

A short message is enough to start. Include these three points:

  1. What is slowing the team down?
  2. Which systems and people are involved?
  3. What result do you need, and by when?
