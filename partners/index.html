Before you apply

Is there a fixed commission?

No standard commission is offered on this page. Any referral compensation, payment trigger and responsibilities require a separate written agreement.

Does submitting make me a partner?

No. The form records an application. A collaboration starts only after fit, responsibilities and terms have been agreed. Leads, revenue and exclusivity are not guaranteed.

Who owns the customer and the work?

The partner keeps its customer relationship. The contracting party, communication rules, rights to existing materials and newly created work are defined in the agreement; there is no automatic transfer.

Can I share client documents?

For the first message, describe the situation without confidential client information, credentials or personal data about other people. Agree a secure exchange and confidentiality terms when needed.
