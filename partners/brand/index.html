Keep the files intact

  1. Proportions and clear space

    Do not stretch, rotate, outline or add a shadow or other effect. Leave clear space of one quarter of the logo height on every side. Nothing else goes in that space.

  2. Do not recolor

    Do not rebuild the mark. Do not use a gradient logo or the 2025 logo.

  3. Minimum size

    Use the full logo at 120 pixels or more on screen, and at 40 mm or more in print. Below that, leave it off. Do not redraw a smaller icon.

How to describe the relationship

After Edge has agreed in writing, you may name the relationship in your own materials. You may not present your firm as Edge.

You may say

“Partner of Edge.” With the agreed track: “Referral partner of Edge”, “Delivery partner of Edge” or “Technology partner of Edge.”

You may not

Use Edge as your company name, call yourself an Edge office, or say that you can bind the company.

Product name

In English and in Russian, EdgeFocus stays in Latin and in one word.

Permission, contracts and payment

Written permission

Using the Edge name or logo requires prior written permission from ООО «ЭДЖ ЭКОСИСТЕМА». Submitting the partner form does not grant that permission.

There is no standard commission. Any referral compensation needs its own written agreement and is not published here.

  1. Do not act for Edge

    Do not sign contracts, issue invoices, collect payments or make commitments on behalf of Edge.

  2. Clients pay the company

    Clients pay only to the official bank details of ООО «ЭДЖ ЭКОСИСТЕМА» published on the company page. Do not pay a partner account, a personal account or any other recipient. Match the recipient to that legal name, INN 9707054215 and OGRN 1257700561590 before paying.

  3. Verify and report

    To confirm a partner, or to report misuse of the name, the logo or a payment request, email hello@edgeivaikin.com or write on Telegram. A logo on a page or in a message is not proof.
