Cultural platform · 2023–2024

Discover Culture · Saudi Ministry of Culture

Context
The Ministry needed a single place to find cultural events and reserve tickets.
What we built
Timothy Ivaikin contributed as a technical lead to the platform’s development. He worked on the project through Enlightened Ventures LLC-FZ, his Dubai company within Edge.
Result
The Ministry announced the beta on 23 November 2023. Discover Culture handled ticketing for the Bienalsur exhibition in Riyadh and registration for the 2024 Madinah Book Fair.
