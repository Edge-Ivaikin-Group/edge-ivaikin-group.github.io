Registration and regulatory portals

An economic development programme needed software for two related jobs: registering participants and handling regulatory procedures online. Edge delivered components for both.

Programme
Economic development
Delivery scope
Registration, compliance and regulatory portals
Team
Edge delivery team. Timothy Ivaikin led the company.

What the delivery covered

Edge’s part of the programme covered three areas.

Registration systems
Systems for registering participants in the programme.
Compliance workflows
Workflows for carrying out compliance procedures digitally.
Regulatory portals
Separate portal components for regulatory procedures beyond initial registration.

Scoping a similar portal

Start with one procedure. Define what the applicant submits, who reviews it, who makes the decision and what record must be kept. Test that full path with applicants and reviewers before extending the portal to other procedures.
